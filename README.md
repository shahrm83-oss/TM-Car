# RC car for Terrier Motorsport onboarding

Every new mechanical member of Terrier Motorsport, Boston University's Formula SAE team, designs and builds a small battery-powered car from workshop stock. This repo holds my design files.

**Full write-up:** [shahrm83-oss.github.io/rc-car.html](https://shahrm83-oss.github.io/rc-car.html)

## Brief
- Chassis limited to 120 × 120 mm
- At least one part laser-cut, one milled, one 3D-printed and one turned
- Two weeks to manufacture, using stock and tools from BU's EPIC workshop
- Goal: a light, fast car that is easy to manufacture

## Design
Hanging aero cover, clamp-fitted motor, elevated battery mount, sprocket drive and 30 mm wheels.

After the critical design review I:
1. Raised the aero mount so the battery mount fits underneath
2. Redesigned the motor mount
3. Lowered the motor mount to bring the motor closer to the wheels

## Manufacturing
| Part | Process |
|---|---|
| Motor mount | CNC mill, from my own Fusion 360 CAM toolpaths |
| Wheels | Manual lathe |
| Battery mount | 3D printed |

## What I learned
- Taking a part from CAD to CAM to a finished part
- Planning time across weeks rather than days
- Spotting easy oversights early
- Using the manual mill, manual lathe, laser cutter and CNC mill
